## What should you check for the Mission 6/7 reviewer?

In the [October 9 Mission 6/7 thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3232690/discussions/0/581684470096302169/), the poster had already raised cafe level and recipe count. Another player advised talking to the reviewer outside at night; the conversation says the visit will be tomorrow and identifies a drink to prepare. The poster thanked the reply.

If the same symptom matches your current objective, check for that conversation. Read the drink request in your own game: the thread does not establish an exact recipe we can reproduce here. The cafe-level and recipe counts in the question are the poster's situation, not confirmed minimum requirements.

See [progression and Maidchelin visits](/guides/progression/) for the official scope of the rating system.

## Where is the promo-photo camera?

In the [promo-photo discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3232690/discussions/0/581684714275917642/), a reply points to the staff room with red curtains after the cafe closes at the end of the day. The poster acknowledged not waiting until closing.

Check that timing before assuming the camera is missing. The developer's announcement separately describes Performance Stage filming every fifth night and a photo after the performance. The community post concerns a specific promo-photo task; it should not be read as permission to film on every night.

## Why can you not put the fifth maid to work?

The [fifth-maid discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3232690/discussions/0/581684470096304950/) reports a four-worker limit. A reply recommends expanding the main hall. The poster then reported that upgrading the hall from level 6 to level 9 resolved the problem.

The reply mentions level 8, but the follow-up only confirms the successful level-9 state. It does not independently prove an exact unlock at level 8. Check the displayed expansion and capacity requirements rather than relying on an unverified threshold.

The [maid roster reference](/reference/maids-and-bonds/) distinguishes joining the story from having room for an additional worker.

## Is there a confirmed fix for the Chapter 10 stall?

No fix is established by the collected [Chapter 10 report](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3232690/discussions/0/581684714275927545/). The poster describes starting a new full-game save, expanding both rooms beyond the stated requirements and waiting several days without an evening event. No answer was present when this thread was collected on October 10.

One report does not establish a universal defect. This guide has no verified reset, save-editing or reinstall procedure for it. Deleting saves would be an unsupported response to the evidence currently available.

## How should you report an unresolved problem?

The [October 13 developer notice](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067943316) identifies **F8** or the **ESC menu** as in-game bug-reporting options. The [developer's Discord](https://discord.gg/dv2xNkvrpD) remains a support-community link from the launch announcement. For a useful report, describe the current objective, full-game or demo build, the time and location checked, and the steps that reproduce the symptom. Those details are an editorial reporting checklist, not claimed game mechanics.
