## Do demo saves currently carry into the full game?

**A confirmed transfer issue is currently affecting demo saves.** In the [October 13 developer notice](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067943316), Accelix says the promised transfer is not working correctly and a fix is being prepared. Its interim advice is to **start a new game** in the full release.

No fix release date or manual recovery method is published in that notice. It does not instruct players to delete existing saves.

The [October 8 launch announcement](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067928563) promised transfer using a slot labeled **[Demo]**. That instruction is historical; selecting the slot does not establish that the current transfer works. No successful firsthand transfer test is claimed.

## What changes when you move from the demo to the full game?

| Scope | Launch demo | Full game |
|---|---|---|
| Main story | First four chapters | Remaining chapters, an ending sequence and credits |
| Cafe level cap | 10 | 40 |
| Additional maids | Demo starts with Omu and Mina | Yuki, Jennie and Momo join as introduced by the main story |
| Additional systems | Limited demo scope | Inspections, festivals, expanded rooms and two stage systems |

The full-game maid level cap is 15. Read the [progression guide](/guides/progression/) for the distinction between Dance Stage work and Performance Stage filming.

## Why did an older demo save stop working?

The official September 29 demo update, version **1.16.22**, says the demo's save system was rewritten and earlier demo saves were incompatible with that updated demo. That historical reset is a separate event from the full-game launch's promise to carry demo saves forward.

Do not treat version 1.16.22 as the full game's release version. The same demo update also changed demo features and limits, so a June demo guide may describe a different build.

Source: the September 29 entry in the [official Steam news feed](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=3232690&count=50&maxlength=0&format=json), preserved in this project's source records.

## How do you report a transfer problem?

The October 13 notice identifies **F8** or the **ESC menu** as in-game bug-reporting options. Include the demo build, full-game build and what happens when you try the save; those details are an editorial reporting checklist. The notice does not supply a missing-slot recovery procedure or a save-file editing method.

The [developer's support community](https://discord.gg/dv2xNkvrpD) is another place to follow official announcements. A follow-up announcing the fix would justify changing the current advice; no such fix is established by the sources checked for this update.

For other stalled objectives, use the [story troubleshooting guide](/guides/troubleshooting/). Its community checks do not establish a fix for this transfer issue or the unresolved Chapter 10 report. For cafe operations, see the [beginner walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough/).

## Which release does this guide refer to?

The developer announced the full release on October 8, 2026. The US Steam store date can appear as October 7; date presentation differs from the announcement. This guide was revised on October 13 because the developer confirmed a material transfer issue; the older demo change and launch promise remain historical context.
