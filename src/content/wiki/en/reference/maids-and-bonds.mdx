## Who are the five maids?

The full-game roster is **Omu, Mina, Yuki, Jennie and Momo**. Omu and Mina appear in the updated demo; the [launch announcement](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067928563) says Yuki, Jennie and Momo join as the main story introduces them.

The following descriptions summarize the character blurbs in each maid's official outfit DLC page. They describe personality, not worker stats or a performance tier list.

| Maid | Officially described personality | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Omu | Polite and dependable, with a tiger's instincts underneath | [Omu outfit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4786460/) |
| Mina | Instinct-led and loyal once she warms to you | [Mina outfit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4817670/) |
| Yuki | Cool and aloof at first, with a clumsy side as you get closer | [Yuki outfit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4817800/) |
| Jennie | Tough language that masks a more vulnerable side | [Jennie outfit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4818010/) |
| Momo | Sunny, scatterbrained and guileless | [Momo outfit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4817910/) |

## How do joining and work capacity differ?

Story introduction brings a maid into the roster. The cafe's ability to put another worker on duty is a separate concern. A launch discussion reports that expanding the main hall resolved a fifth-worker capacity problem; it does not establish a fully verified numerical capacity table.

If all five are present but one cannot work, read the [specific staff-capacity check](/guides/troubleshooting/#why-can-you-not-put-the-fifth-maid-to-work). The [progression guide](/guides/progression/) covers the confirmed full-game level caps and expansion systems.

## What do the sources confirm about bond stories?

The Steam store lists **36 bond stories**, and the developer says all five characters' bond stories continue to their endings in the full game. Steam also publishes an achievement for seeing the ending of each maid's story.

This does not supply each bond event's required level, preferred food, gift quantity or time window. It also does not prove a system of mutually exclusive romance routes. Those details need a reproducible full-game demonstration or explicit developer instructions before they can be presented as a route.

The store and launch announcement give different totals for late-night text stories. This page does not combine those figures or turn either count into an unlock requirement.

## Are outfits and Maidroids shared across all characters?

Official DLC entries identify the supported maid. An outfit for Omu does not include the matching outfit for another character. The launch catalog contains three outfit entries and one signature Maidroid per maid; the latter is a helper with assignable cafe jobs.

Use the [DLC comparison](/reference/dlc/) to inspect the character-specific item before purchasing. Owning an outfit is not evidence of a bond-story unlock, and no DLC requirement for completing the base game is claimed.
