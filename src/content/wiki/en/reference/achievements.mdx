## How many Steam achievements are there?

The [Steam achievement page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/3232690/achievements/?l=english) lists **23 entries** for App 3232690. Twenty have public descriptions; three hide their requirements. The table below paraphrases the visible objectives as checked on October 10, 2026.

## What is a useful way to organize your checklist?

Use the public objectives to group your own progress. This is an editorial tracking order, not a tested speedrun route:

1. **Opening and service:** the sign, first sale, first discovery and exact-change streak.
2. **Business milestones:** one-drink sales, game-table fees, cumulative sales and a festival day's revenue. Keep each counter's wording separate; cumulative sales are not profit.
3. **Exploration and collection:** distance, recipes and the explicit vending-machine action.
4. **Maid stories:** gifts, favorite foods, closeness and each character's story ending. Consult the [maid and bond reference](/reference/maids-and-bonds/) for what the official materials establish.
5. **Maidchelin and hidden entries:** use the in-game requirements. An exact three-star route and the three hidden triggers are still awaiting reliable evidence.

The [beginner walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough/) explains the service loop. The [progression reference](/guides/progression/) distinguishes festivals, Dance Stage tips and Performance Stage filming.

## Does the ending achievement prove there are multiple main endings?

No. Its published objective concerns the endings of **all five maid stories**. The developer's [launch announcement](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067928563) mentions a main-game ending sequence and credits, but does not establish a branching list of main endings or their triggers.
