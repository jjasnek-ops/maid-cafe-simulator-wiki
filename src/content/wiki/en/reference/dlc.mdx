## Do you need DLC to complete the game?

No. In the [launch DLC discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3232690/discussions/0/581684470096185808/), Accelix, marked as the developer, says all DLC is optional and the complete base game can be played without it.

The launch reply discussed possible bundles. A later [October 13 developer announcement](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067942612) gives concrete bundle and free-outfit plans, summarized below.

## Which bundles and free outfits were announced on October 13?

The developer lists three five-maid collections for October 13:

- **Jersey Maid Uniform Collection**
- **Black Sugar Bunny Collection**
- **Signature Costume Collection**

The announced bundle reduction is 15% against the included items' combined regular prices; existing owners pay for the remaining items. This is the developer's announced structure. Check the current Steam listing for your region and ownership-specific total; this guide has not verified every live bundle listing.

The same notice promises **two free outfits usable by all five maids**, planned within October for owners of the base game regardless of DLC ownership. The exact release schedule is still to be announced. These are planned additions, not a confirmed released outfit update or another five-character paid bundle.

## How do outfit purchases differ from Maidroids?

**Outfits are character-specific.** The official product descriptions identify the maid supported and state that other characters' outfits are not included. Buying Black Sugar Bunny for Omu does not buy the Mina, Yuki, Momo or Jennie version.

**Signature Maidroids are permanent purchased helpers.** Their product descriptions describe choosing cafe jobs and changing assignments on the whiteboard, including service and kitchen work. Calling every DLC cosmetic would therefore misrepresent the catalog.

These descriptions do not provide a verified productivity ranking, payback period or numerical advantage against every base-game worker. This page compares what is included, not an invented best-value tier list.

## How can you compare items before deciding?

1. Check the **maid name** in the product title.
2. Check whether the item is an **outfit or a Maidroid**.
3. Open the linked Steam product page to confirm the current region price and availability.
4. If you want to finish the story without extras, remember the developer's explicit statement that DLC is optional.
